Kaise use karein?

  1. 📖 Learn: Level 0 se shuru karo. Har word pe 🔊 dabao aur zor se bolo.
  2. 👂 Listen: Awaaz suno aur sahi meaning chuno. Isse kaan train honge.
  3. 🎤 Speak: Mic mein bolo, website batayegi sahi bola ya nahi.
  4. 🔁 Translate: Koi word yaad nahi aa raha? English ya Japanese mein search karo.
  5. ✍️ Scribble: Likhna seekho (thoda aage jaake).
  6. 🔁 Revision: Flashcards aur quiz ke cards sahi din pe dohraane ke liye wapas aate hain (Learn page pe).
  7. 🧠 Tricks: Jaldi seekhne ke tareeke, word endings, katakana rules, kanji radicals.
